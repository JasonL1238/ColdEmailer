## Free Quotas (More than enough for cold emails)

### Daily Limits (FREE)
- **Send emails**: 500 emails per day (per user)
- **Read emails**: 1 billion quota units per day
- **Other operations**: Very generous limits

### What This Means for You

Your app is configured with:

- **Rate limiting**: 1 email per 3 seconds

This is **well within** the free tier:

- ✅ You'll never hit the free quota
- ✅ **No charges ever**
